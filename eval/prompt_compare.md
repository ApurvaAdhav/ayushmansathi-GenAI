# AyushmanSathi – Prompt Comparison

## 1. Evaluation Overview

- **Version A:** Basic Prompt (`task1`)
- **Version B:** Improved Prompt (`task2`)
- **Model:** Gemini API
- **API Endpoint:** `POST /chat`
- **Evaluation Method:** Both prompts are evaluated using the same 10 healthcare questions.
- **Response Format:** Answer, confidence score, and follow-up question.
- **Total Test Cases:** 20 (10 questions × 2 prompt versions).

The objective is to compare the response quality, clarity, accuracy, context usage, and follow-up suggestions generated by the two prompts.

---

## 2. Prompt Evaluation Results

### Question 1: What is the maximum health coverage amount provided under the PM-JAY scheme?

**Version A – Basic Prompt (`task1`)**

- **Answer:** Under the PM-JAY scheme, eligible beneficiary families receive health coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation at empanelled hospitals.
- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Follow-up:** Would you like to know which treatments are covered under this scheme?

**Version B – Improved Prompt (`task2`)**

- **Answer:** The Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides eligible families with health coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year. This coverage supports eligible secondary and tertiary hospitalisation expenses at empanelled hospitals, according to scheme guidelines.
- **Confidence:** 0.98
- **Follow-up:** Would you like to understand how beneficiaries can use this ₹5 lakh coverage?

### Question 4: What is pre-authorisation, and why is it required before certain treatments?

**Version A – Basic Prompt (`task1`)**

- **Answer:** Pre-authorisation is a process that requires prior approval and submission of specific pre- and post-operative investigations, such as X-rays, ultrasound reports, blood tests, and scan photographs, for certain treatments and packages. It is required for specific treatments such as cancer care, cardiac, radiation, and surgical oncology treatments to determine the best course of treatment, manage costs, and avoid unnecessary treatments. In emergency cases, pre-authorisation may not be required initially, but formal intimation may be completed within 24 hours of admission.
- **Confidence:** 0.95
- **Follow-up:** Would you like to know more about the pre-authorisation requirements for cancer care procedures?

**Version B – Improved Prompt (`task2`)**

- **Answer:** Pre-authorisation refers to getting prior approval for certain treatments, such as cancer care and other specialised medical packages. For cancer care, it may be required at each step to go through a clinical approval process. This helps assess the best course of treatment and manage costs.
- **Confidence:** 0.85
- **Follow-up:** Is pre-authorisation required for emergency or lifesaving procedures?

Additionally, if there is a delay in response due to technical issues, a pre-authorisation code can be obtained through the phone from the Insurance Company, Trust, or call centre, and required documents can be sent later through the transaction system.

**Version B – Improved Prompt (`task2`)**
